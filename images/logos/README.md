# Logos folder

Place the three required logos here, all with a **white** background:

| File name                | Content                                    | Recommended size  |
|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| `republic_tunisia.png`   | Republic of Tunisia coat of arms (white bg)| 600 x 600 px      |
| `dget.png`               | DGET / Ministry of Higher Education logo   | 600 x 600 px      |
| `iset_siliana.png`       | ISET Siliana logo                          | 600 x 600 px      |

The cover page (`chapters/cover.tex`) renders the three logos at a **fixed
height of 2.8 cm** inside a 3 cm white box, so they all appear visually
balanced regardless of their original aspect ratio.

## Why a white background ?

The Tunisian PFE guide and most ISET cover-page conventions require a clean
white backdrop behind official logos. Make sure each PNG is exported with
a **transparent or fully white** background — never a colored or textured
backdrop.
